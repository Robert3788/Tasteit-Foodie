2. What Data We Collect

When you create an account:

  • Email address
  • Encrypted password (we never see your password in plain text)

In your profile:

  • Username
  • Profile bio, if you provide one
  • Profile picture, if you upload one

When using the app:

  • Recipes you create (title, description, ingredients, preparation steps, categories)
  • Images you upload
  • Likes you give
  • Comments and replies you write
  • Follow relationships (who you follow, who follows you)
  • Reports you submit about content
  • Technical usage data (e.g. account creation time, timestamps of actions)

We do not collect location data, contact lists, or payment information (as the app currently has no paid features).

3. Purposes of Data Processing

We process your data to:

  • create and manage your account
  • provide the app's core features (sharing recipes, following, commenting, liking)
  • automatically analyze uploaded recipe images using artificial intelligence to estimate nutritional information
  • review reported content and moderate violations
  • operate, secure, and improve the app

4. Legal Basis

Processing is based on your consent given at registration (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and on the performance of the user agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), where applicable.

5. Processors and Recipients

To operate Tasteit, we use the following service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase (database, authentication, file storage) – processes account data, profile data, and content
  • Anthropic (AI image analysis) – processes uploaded recipe images to estimate nutritional information
  • Cloudinary (image optimization and storage) – processes uploaded images
  • Resend (email delivery) – processes email addresses to send system notifications

We have appropriate data processing agreements in place with these providers. Data may be transferred outside your country of residence as part of using these services; in such cases, we ensure appropriate safeguards (e.g. standard contractual clauses) are in place.

6. Retention Period

We retain your data for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, your personal data will be deleted within 30 days, unless we are required by law to retain it longer. Content that other users have already saved, shared, or commented on may remain to a limited extent.

You can delete your account and all associated data directly through the app settings, or contact us at info@foodieswipe.com.

8. Data Security

We implement technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse, including encryption in transit and access restrictions on our systems.

9. Children
